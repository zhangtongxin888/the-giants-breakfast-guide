Official cooking loop

  1. Buy giant food. Pull the lever to purchase a giant food item. Official sources do not publish exact prices—use the values shown in your live session.
  2. Choose the right station. Put batters, eggs, and meat on the griddle. Put bread and pastries in the toaster.
  3. Wait for it to cook. Once an item is cooked, it pops up. Exact cook times are not published in the official description reviewed for this guide.
  4. Build the tower. The cooked item joins your stack. Each stacked item earns money every second.

Quick FAQ

What is the door code in The Giant's Breakfast?

Community sources commonly try EGGS on the in-world lab/door keypad. That is separate from promo gift codes in the Codes UI. Full steps live on the door-code page.

What is the giants breakfast roblox door code?

Same answer: enter EGGS on the in-world lab or door keypad—not in an external redeem site and not as a Discord gift string unless Zidiam also publishes it as a promo code.

How do you play The Giant's Breakfast?

Pull the lever to buy food, cook on the griddle or toaster, stack the tower for money per second, and climb toward rarer food including Celestial.

Are there promo codes?

No official active promo codes were verified against the official Roblox listing and independent code trackers reviewed for this guide. We do not invent a code list.

Does EGGS work as a promo or Discord code?

Treat EGGS as an in-world puzzle candidate. Independent trackers and this wiki keep it off the promo table unless Zidiam publishes it as a gift string separately.

Where do I enter the door / lab code?

On the in-world keypad, terminal, chalkboard, or task field near the clue—inside the Roblox experience. Do not paste it into third-party redeem websites.

How do you get Celestials in The Giant's Breakfast?

Keep growing your tower. The official description says bigger stacks find rarer food up to the Celestial tier, so Celestial food comes from the same lever after your stack is tall enough. It still cooks on the normal station: batters, eggs and meat on the griddle, bread and pastries in the toaster. Exact unlock heights and odds are not published.

What is the rarest food tier?

Celestial is the highest rarity named in the official Roblox description. The full ordered ladder and drop rates are not published there.
