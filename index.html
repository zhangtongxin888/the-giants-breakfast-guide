THE OFFICIAL LOOP

Four moves. One enormous tower.

The game's official description gives a simple loop. These are the mechanics it names—without invented recipes, prices, or odds.

01

Pull

Use a lever to buy a giant food item.

02

Cook

Use the griddle for batter, eggs, and meat, or the toaster for bread and pastries.

03

Stack

Cooked food pops up and joins your growing tower.

04

Earn

Every stacked item makes money each second; a taller tower unlocks rarer food.
